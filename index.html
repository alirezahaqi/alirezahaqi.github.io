Alireza Haqi

PhD Student in Computer Science

Alireza Haqi is a Computer Science PhD student at Stanford University, supervised by Prof. Moses Charikar and Prof. Nima Anari. His research is in theoretical computer science, with a focus on parallel and distributed algorithms, randomized sampling, graph optimization, and rounding.
